Ramp Stack

June 2026 · Online

The premise

An accountant can judge finished work. Ramp introduced Stack with a more demanding question than “does this AI sound clever?”: can it complete accounting tasks? The benchmark gave buyers something closer to their own standard of proof.

How it launched

Ramp launched Stack for accounting firms in June 2026 with an explainer, a second launch film, customer examples and a published evaluation. Accountants wrote and graded more than 200 tasks for the benchmark.

The films explained the offer; the benchmark gave a skeptical viewer somewhere to investigate. For a product that asks professionals to trust its work, the demonstration and the test deserve as much attention as the headline.

Reported results

Ramp published a benchmark of 200+ accountant-written and graded tasks. Its launch article also quoted firms closing books 50% faster. These describe the product and customer experience. Launch article · Benchmark
