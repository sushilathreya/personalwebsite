Incentives: reward the behavior

August 2025 – November 2026

The premise

A prize can buy a click. The rules decide what happens next. Ramp’s offers are more useful to study side by side: some pay for a conversation, others for product use, approval or repeated attendance.

How it launched

These promotions used different routes, including website behavior, paid ads, partner webinars and speaker posts. Each paired an offer with a specific action. Read the required action before judging the size of the reward.

The trade-off is who the offer attracts and how far they must go to qualify. A memorable gift can start the conversation; an activation requirement asks the buyer to put real work into the product.
