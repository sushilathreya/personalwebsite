$500,000 Treasury Sweepstakes

August 2026 · Online

  • Campaign landing page ↗

The premise

A $500,000 prize is difficult to ignore. The more interesting work happened in the entry rules: open an account, hold a balance, pay a bill. The promotion introduced prospects to the actions Ramp wanted them to repeat as customers.

How it launched

Ramp ran the sweepstakes from August to October 2026. It offered entries for an approved new checking account, available balances and a qualifying first bill payment, alongside a free mail-in route. The rules connected the prize to specific product actions.

After the promotion closed, Ramp named Kalo as the winner and published co-founder Marc Perkins’s plans for the grant. That gave the campaign an ending people could see. The prize bought attention; the mechanics made that attention relevant to the product.
