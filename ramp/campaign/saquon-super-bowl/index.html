Expenses Should Do Themselves

February 2025 · US national

  • Behind the scenes
  • Saquon Barkley with receipts
  • Ramp card campaign still

The premise

Put a pile of receipts in front of Saquon Barkley and the waste of time is immediately obvious. Every second he spends sorting paper is a second you’d rather see him spend on the field. That’s the expense automation pitch in one image.

How it launched

Ramp paired the 15-second Super Bowl spot with news that Barkley was investing in the company. Then it published the making-of, giving the fast production its own story after the broadcast. The investment gave reporters a second reason to cover a very short ad.

Reported results

Campaign reporting

In a March 4, 2025 TBPN interview, Ramp CEO Eric Glyman described February 2025 as Ramp’s strongest February to date and reported directional increases in sales and willingness to recommend. He withheld figures. He said the last pregame placement aired nationally and some markets saw the spot twice. Ramp’s official launch and Even/Odd’s production case study establish the ad and partnership, with no quantified campaign-wide outcomes published on either page.
