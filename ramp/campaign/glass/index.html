Introducing Glass

June 2026 – October 2026 · Online

The premise

“We needed this ourselves” is easy to say. Ramp launched Glass with details of its own use: employees editing shared knowledge, capturing customer requests and sending updates. Those examples made the origin story inspectable before the early-access invitation.

How it launched

CEO Eric Glyman introduced Glass in October 2026 by connecting Ramp’s internal AI work to a shared knowledge tool. The launch described what people had already done with it, then invited other businesses to join early access.

The useful sequence is problem, working example, invitation. Internal usage is evidence that the team has lived with the problem; early-access signups tell a different story about external interest.

Another way to sell what the team learned

Jun 2026

Ramp’s June 2026 Applied AI Solutions launch offered customers help applying the experience behind its internal AI work. Glass turns that experience into a tool; Applied AI Solutions offers help doing the work. Both use the company’s own experience as the opening proof.
