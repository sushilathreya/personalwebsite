Sports partnerships: beyond the logo

January 2026 – October 2026

The premise

A sports logo earns recognition. The finance work behind a team can earn a business buyer’s interest. Ramp’s partnerships combine those jobs in different ways, and the distinction is worth keeping visible.

How it launched

Tennessee paired athlete attention with an institutional customer film. The Broncos story focused on finance operations. The 49ers agreement combined a customer relationship with stadium, digital and event rights.

The strongest material shows something a finance team can recognize in its own work. Rights promised in a deal matter, but they become a marketing example when someone actually uses them.
