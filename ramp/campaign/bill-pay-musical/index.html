The premise

Paying bills is spectacularly untheatrical. Ramp made that mismatch the joke and backed it with a real Broadway production. They wrote original songs, choreography, and hired a professional cast which made the show much more than a marketing gimmick.

How it launched

A campaign microsite offered free registration. Rehearsal access, the arrival experience and permission to record gave press and attendees material to share from different parts of the evening. The campaign site later switched to promoting London, so the New York production is documented separately.

Reported results

Ramp announced the New York production on September 10 and held the free, one-night show at the Broadhurst Theatre on September 25, 2026. The same microsite and social handles now support a distinct West End production announced for December 14, 2026 at London’s Dominion Theatre. Every observation below refers to the New York production. TheaterMania announcement, Broadway & Beyond executive producer production record, InsideHook rehearsal review, official West End microsite
