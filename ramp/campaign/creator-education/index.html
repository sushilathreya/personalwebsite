The premise

A useful Excel lesson can earn an hour that a product pitch never would. Ramp brought in finance practitioners to teach work its audience already needed to do. The expertise gave people a reason to register; a recurring program gave them a reason to return.

How it launched

Sessions included Nicolas Boucher and Christian Martinez on a Claude finance assistant, Paul Barnhurst on data visualization, Carl Seidman on dynamic Excel and Josh Aharonoff on an Excel coach. Each promised a concrete skill and linked to a registration or replay page.

Notice how close the subjects sit to the buyer’s job without requiring every lesson to be about Ramp. The teacher, the skill and the replay make a useful unit of content. One can lead naturally to the next session.
