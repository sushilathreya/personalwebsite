The premise

What if we took a simple (slightly silly) idea and turned it into a whole world? Take a man famous for loving ramps and put him in a Ramp ad. Tony Hawk has to give up skating and start trading stocks under witness protection - giving birth to "Tony Stock".

How it launched

The episodes led into an interactive safe house, complete with case files, Ramp XP and a RampBerry. The site gave people more of the world they’d just watched. Ramp hosted an invitation-only finale at the New York Stock Exchange with Tony Hawk and Bam Margera.
