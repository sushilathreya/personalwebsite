Product launches: make the work visible

July 2025 – September 2026

The premise

Ramp repeatedly starts with a task finance teams recognize: review a purchase, process an invoice, close the books, get paid. Comparing the launches shows how a broad automation promise becomes a specific job a buyer can judge.

How it launched

Launch articles, films, employee posts and demos introduced the products. The strongest examples paired the claim with a workflow, customer or test that helped a buyer investigate it.

Watch for the distance between the opening promise and the proof. Product benchmarks explain what the tool can do. Post views explain how a particular piece of marketing traveled. They answer different questions.
