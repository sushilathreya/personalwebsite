The premise

Ramp had something most marketing teams cannot manufacture: a live view of what businesses spend. The AI Index turned that data into news people could cite. Each update gave journalists and researchers a fresh reason to return, even if they had no intention of buying Ramp.

How it launched

Ramp introduced the index with its April 2025 Spring Spending Report, then published recurring charts, commentary, downloadable data and a newsletter. The repeatable asset was the changing number, rather than a new campaign idea every month.

By October 2026, the St. Louis Fed had added 14 Ramp AI adoption series to FRED. Getting the data into a reference system extended its life beyond Ramp’s own posts. The newsletter gave interested readers somewhere to subscribe.

A report buyers can take into a meeting

Jun 2025

The June 2025 State of Procurement report served a different job. Its survey put numbers behind manual work and poor spend visibility, giving finance leaders evidence for an internal argument. Compare the two: the report helps a buyer make a case; the index gives the press a changing story to follow.
