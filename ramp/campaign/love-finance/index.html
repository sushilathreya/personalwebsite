Love Finance

June 2025 · US national

  • Fixing the Parts of Finance You Hate
  • Closing the Books

The premise

Finance workers do not need a brand to tell them their profession is miserable. “Love Finance” gave them permission to enjoy the job and made the administrative friction the enemy. That choice gave separate product stories a shared emotional point of view.

How it launched

Ramp worked with Arts & Letters on three June 2025 films: Chasing Receipts, Fixing the Parts of Finance You Hate and Closing the Books. Broadcast, streaming and social carried the work.

Watch the films together. Chasing receipts and closing the books give the promise a recognizable task, rather than asking a general claim about “time back” to carry the ad. The same attitude can connect several features without making every film recite the product list.
