Treasury has landed

January 2025 · New York

  • The completed installation
  • Social reveal ↗

The premise

High returns or easy access to your cash? Ramp made the word “or” the villain. Smashing it into an ampersand made an abstract treasury pitch readable from across the street.

How it launched

The Manhattan wall went up with “OR” intact. Then came the cracked-wall effect, lime wrecking ball and crushed car. The two versions gave passersby and social viewers a before-and-after story they could retell in seconds.

The street-interview video gave the installation a second audience online. The wall did the explaining before anyone needed to talk about treasury. That is the useful trick: build the product objection into something a phone camera can capture.
