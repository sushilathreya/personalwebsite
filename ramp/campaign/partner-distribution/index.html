Partnerships: build a route to the buyer

March 2025 – September 2026

The premise

A partnership earns its place when it changes how a buyer hears about, pays for or benefits from a product. These three relationships solve different parts of that journey.

How it launched

SMU invited its business community into a referral relationship. Ingram Micro and Microsoft added sales support and buying routes. Universal Ads put an advertising offer in front of existing Ramp customers.

The logo is the announcement. The mechanism is the lesson: identify the audience, the offer and the step that becomes easier because the partner is involved.
