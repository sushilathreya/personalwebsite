The premise

After Brian’s Office, the audience already knew what one Brian looked like at work. Ramp cloned him for its Super Bowl ad to make a different point: automation lets one team do the work of many.

How it launched

Ramp extended the TV spot with ten original social pieces, appearances by Andy Buckley, a bald-cap giveaway, and a Brian lookalike contest. Two winners got game tickets, and the stadium trip was livestreamed. The bald cap let fans become part of the joke with almost no explanation.

Reported results

Ramp released the Super Bowl LX commercial on January 27, 2026. The San Francisco tailgate and game took place on February 8. Ramp campaign archive, original commercial
