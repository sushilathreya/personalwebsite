Router.com

August 2026 · Online

The premise

Developers choose models repeatedly, and every choice affects cost. Router put Ramp’s spending promise at that decision point. A useful tool gave the brand a way into a technical audience’s daily work.

How it launched

Ramp opened Router to external builders in August 2026 with a demo and introductory model credits and discounts. The launch asked people to try a task they already understood: route work between models.

The offer lowered the cost of trying the tool, while the demo showed the action. That is a more concrete invitation than a general claim that Ramp understands AI. The marketing question is whether a useful first experience earns another visit.
