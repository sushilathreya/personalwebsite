The premise

Kumar appeared out of nowhere: a swaggering accountant whose videos went viral and left everyone asking for his method. Then he disappeared. Was he a Ramp plant all along? Nobody knows. By the time Ramp appeared, the audience already had a mystery to solve: where had Kumar gone, and would he ever release his method? A book on Ramp’s site finally gave that running joke somewhere to go.

How it launched

Kumar came back in a cinematic Instagram reel, forgave his enemies and pointed viewers to a ten-chapter book. Ramp co-authored the reels and maintained the mystery in its own recap. The partnership is documented. Who ran the account in its earliest days remains unclear.

Reported results

Campaign-level claim

Ramp’s Creative Experimentation archive reports 300M+ views. The page describes Kumar’s August 2026 return and embeds two co-authored Instagram reels and a carousel. The figure is a Ramp-reported campaign headline. The campaign site’s count of 10 chapters describes the content released. Ramp archive, campaign microsite.

Before Ramp appeared

  • The Kumar-branded YouTube channel’s June 5 debut had 62,126 views and 1,764 likes on YouTube as of November 23, 2026. On November 23, the Instagram account showed 1M followers and 15 posts, both account-level figures. YouTube debut, Kumar’s Instagram profile.
  • Ramp released the book and co-authored reel on August 17.
