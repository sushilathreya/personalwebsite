OnRamp: the event and its afterlife

September 2025 – September 2026 · New York, San Francisco

  • Session playlist ↗

The premise

A customer conference gives a product roadmap people, arguments and a room to land in. OnRamp put practitioners beside Ramp’s leaders, then turned the sessions into material people could use after the event.

How it launched

Ramp held its inaugural summits in New York and San Francisco in 2025. Customer and industry speakers joined executive presentations and networking. Recaps and session recordings carried the program beyond the room.

Gifting partner Brilliant added an on-site experience and handled later shipments. That is a separate part of the event to study: what attendees do at the venue, and what continues after they leave. Attendance and gift redemption tell different parts of that story.

2026: give people a reason to make the trip

Sep 2026

The next summit ran September 29–October 1 in San Francisco, with keynotes, breakouts, a partner expo and continuing education credit. The credit gave finance professionals a practical reason to ask for time away from work. Recordings and a short keynote cut let the same program serve people who never attended.
