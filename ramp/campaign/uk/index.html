Country launches: the UK and Canada

July 2026 – September 2026 · United Kingdom

The premise

A country launch has two jobs: prove the product understands the local work, and get the news beyond the company account. Ramp used UK-specific finance details and a customer story for the first. A coordinated employee posting wave helped with the second.

How it launched

Ramp announced UK availability in September 2026 after a summer beta. VAT and receipt workflows, plus Fyxer’s account of a faster close, gave the launch local substance. Employees and CEO Eric Glyman carried the announcement onto LinkedIn.

Ghost’s analysis counted 38 employee posts during launch week; 26 used the same campaign line. Repeating the line across real people made the launch recognizable. The useful distinction is between a consistent message and a company account trying to create all the distribution itself.

Canada: make the local details do the work

Jul 2026

The July 2026 Canadian launch emphasized CAD and USD workflows, tax details and Canadian customer evidence, supported by a launch webinar and employee posts. The UK example adds a coordinated distribution wave; Canada shows why the product story also has to sound local. General Ramp customer savings are not proof of a Canadian launch result.
