The premise

If America was going to stop making pennies, Ramp thought the penny deserved a proper send-off. The funeral took an abstract change in how money works and gave people a ritual they already knew how to join.

How it launched

The Partiful invite told guests to bring pennies and dress in Victorian clothes or as Abraham Lincoln. Historical impersonators, a eulogy and a livestream followed. You could attend, dress up, bring an offering or just watch the absurdity unfold.

Reported results

Ramp staged the event at the Lincoln Memorial in Washington, DC, on December 20, 2025.
