Marketing to AI agents

March 2026 · Online

The premise

If an assistant is researching software for a buyer, can an offer survive the summary? Ramp tested that smaller, answerable question. It put an incentive where AI agents could read it and checked whether their answers passed it along.

How it launched

Ramp placed a tracked offer across roughly 50 pages, varied the presentation and repeatedly asked Claude, Perplexity and ChatGPT questions resembling buyer searches. Its incentive terms were updated in March 2026; the Builders write-up followed in May.

The write-up became a second asset: a practical account of what the team tried and observed. The lesson is to measure the next observable step. An assistant mentioning an offer is useful evidence about distribution, but it is not a customer buying the product.
