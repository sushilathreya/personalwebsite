Unfireable Protocol

#personal

Overview

Nobody teaches you the hidden game of work. Most employees don’t know what will help them succeed in their career. There are certain principles that work across roles and industries. This is what the Unfireable Protocol is built to teach.

Why I created Unfireable Protocol

After working with multiple companies over 5 years, I realised that a lot of people don’t know what really matters at work. They index too much on visibility and not on impact. They don’t understand what really matters to the company. So, I created a guide on how to navigate careers. Perfect for people with 0-5 years of experience.

What I learnt

  • AI video generation using Google Flow, Veo3.1, Heygen using the interface and MCP.
  • Fundamentals of content creation - hooks, editing pace, messaging, ICP, positioning
  • Incorporating a company, building checkout flows using Razorpay
  • Challenges of selling products from an anonymous/pseudonymous account
  • Writing compelling landing pages and funnels
